DeliveryKeeper Privacy Policy

Last updated October 7, 2026

The short version

DeliveryKeeper keeps your delivery records on your phone. There is no account, no sign-in, no advertising and no analytics in the app, and we do not run servers that receive your data. We do not sell or share your information.

Information you enter

Shifts, orders, pay, tips, expenses, receipt photos, odometer readings, pickup names, notes, tax report details and your settings are saved in the app’s private storage on your device. They leave your device only when you choose to export, share or back them up.

Location (optional)

If you turn on automatic GPS mileage, DeliveryKeeper collects your precise location to measure business miles during a shift you start, including while the app is closed or not in use and while your screen is locked. Tracking pauses during breaks and waiting, and stops when you end the shift or turn the feature off.

Only running distance totals and the single most recent location point are kept, on your device. DeliveryKeeper does not store your route history and does not send your location anywhere. If you leave GPS mileage off, the app does not request location permission.

Screenshots, photos and screen capture (optional)

When you choose Take photo for an expense, the app asks for camera permission. Receipt photos you take or choose are kept in the app’s private storage and included in backups you create. They are not uploaded or analyzed by a server. PDF and CSV tax reports include expense details but not the receipt photos.

When you choose an offer screenshot, share an image to DeliveryKeeper, or use the Capture offer Shortcut, the text in that image is read on your device. On iPhone, if you turn on screenshot watching, the app checks your newest screenshots only while it is open. On Android, the optional floating button captures a single frame of your screen after you approve the system prompt each time.

Images are not uploaded and DeliveryKeeper does not save screenshots to your photo library. Only the offer text is kept, on your device, until you review or remove it.

On-device text recognition

On Android, text is read with Google’s ML Kit, which runs on your device. Google states that ML Kit may contact Google servers to receive updates and may send Google technical metrics about how the feature performs; it does not send your images or the recognized text. On iPhone, text is read with Apple’s Vision framework on your device.

Exports, backups and sharing

Tax reports (PDF and CSV) and manual backups are created when you ask. If you enable automatic backups, changed records and receipt photos are saved to the folder you choose while the app is open, at most once an hour. The latest seven automatic copies are kept. A cloud folder such as iCloud Drive may upload those files through its provider; its privacy and sync settings apply. DeliveryKeeper does not send backups to our servers. Turning automatic backups off leaves existing copies in your folder.

Your phone’s own backup service (iCloud or your Google account) may include DeliveryKeeper’s data if you have device backups turned on. That is controlled in your phone’s settings.

Apple subscriptions

On iPhone, Apple processes subscription purchases, free-trial eligibility, billing and refunds through your Apple Account. DeliveryKeeper uses Apple’s StoreKit to check verified subscription status on your device and restore purchases. We do not receive your payment card details, and we do not send your delivery records to Apple as part of a purchase. Apple processes purchase information under its own privacy policy.

Cancel or manage your subscription in your Apple Account settings. Canceling a subscription or deleting app data does not cancel the other. Saved delivery records remain on your device, and tax exports remain available after a subscription ends.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. Use Settings → Your data → Delete all data to erase everything DeliveryKeeper has stored, or uninstall the app. Files you previously exported or backed up elsewhere are not affected.

Children

DeliveryKeeper is meant for adults who work as delivery drivers and is not directed to children under 13.
